Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone/iPad, go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, select SwipeBar Pro, and cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

I purchased Pro but don't see it — what now?

Tap "Restore purchases" on the paywall screen. If the issue persists, make sure you're signed in with the same store account and email us.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Profile → Privacy → Delete account, or email support@swipebar.com.tr.

I can't sign in — what should I do?

Check your internet connection and make sure the app is up to date. If it still fails, reach out to us with your email address.

Is SwipeBar free?

Core features are free. An optional SwipeBar Pro subscription unlocks advanced features like unlimited AI cocktail suggestions and more.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. SwipeBar includes content about alcoholic beverages and is intended for users aged 18 and over. Please drink responsibly.
