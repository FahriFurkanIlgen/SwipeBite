Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the SwipeBar mobile app (the "App"), why and how we process it, and your rights. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and (if you sign in with Apple or Google) basic profile information from that provider.
  • In-app content: Group memberships, cocktail likes, your bar cabinet contents, and your matches.
  • Purchase information: Your subscription status (Pro / free). We never see or store your payment card details; payments are processed by the Apple App Store.
  • Technical data: A device notification token (to send notifications) and basic error/usage logs.

2. Why We Process Data

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To provide core features such as the bar cabinet, cocktail suggestions, and matching with friends.
  • To verify subscription status and unlock Pro features.
  • To send notifications (only if you allow it).
  • To improve the App and fix issues.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted providers to deliver the service:

  • Supabase: Secure hosting of account and app data (over HTTPS with row-level security / RLS).
  • OpenAI: For cocktail suggestions and AI features. Only anonymous text is sent; it is not tied to your identity.
  • Expo Notifications: For push notification delivery.
  • RevenueCat & Apple App Store: For subscription management and payment processing.
  • Sign in with Apple / Google: For authentication, if you choose it.

4. Data Retention & Security

Your data is transmitted encrypted over HTTPS and protected with row-level security (RLS) so only records belonging to you and your group are accessible. We retain your data while your account is active; when you delete your account, the associated personal data is permanently deleted within a reasonable period.

5. Deleting Your Account & Data

You can request deletion of your account and all personal data from within the App via Profile → Privacy → Delete account, or by emailing support@swipebar.com.tr. We process requests as soon as possible.

6. Age Requirement

SwipeBar contains content related to alcoholic beverages and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under this age. Please enjoy responsibly.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. For significant changes we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions: support@swipebar.com.tr
